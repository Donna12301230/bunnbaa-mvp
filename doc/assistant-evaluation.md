## Low scores

### C19: a drink and a snack for afternoon tea next Thursday

The assistant explained that the platform only has breakfast (06:00–10:30), lunch (10:30–14:00) and dinner (16:30–20:00) pre-orders and no separate afternoon-tea period, suggested the lunch period with a later pickup slot (for example 12:30–13:00), and listed three bookable drinks and snacks for 22 Oct.

- **Fulfillment 3:** the workaround (pick a later pickup slot) is sensible, but the cards showed an early 11:00–12:00 slot, which contradicts both the request and the assistant's own advice.
